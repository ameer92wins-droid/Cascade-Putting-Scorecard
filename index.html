Cascade Putting Rules

  1. Limit your shots to 6 per hole.
  2. Putting only—no airborne shots.
  3. Please stay off the rocks and out of the water.
  4. If you lose a golf ball, please ask the operator for a new one.
  5. Most importantly, have fun!
